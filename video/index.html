Before these run

  • The voice is a computer-generated stand-in, there so the words and the pacing can be judged. Have a person record the script; the picture is then cut to the recording.
  • Every platform screen in the videos is an illustration and is labelled as one, because the platform is still being finished.
  • The videos carry the same notices as the site: not an offer, and stem cell therapies have not been approved by the FDA.
  • They are part of the pre-launch draft, so they go to counsel with the rest of it.
