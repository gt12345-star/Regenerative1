190 words. The voice ends at about 1:08.
Read it at an even, unhurried pace, the way you would explain it to one person across a table.
Each time is where that line starts in the current cut. Record the lines in order, with a short pause
between them; the picture is then cut to the recording, so the times do not have to match exactly.
Read the words as written. Do not add figures, results or promises.
