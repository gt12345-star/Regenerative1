Common Questions

All questions

Do I need new space or equipment?

Not much. You already have the office. Regenerative care needs a room where a clinician can see patients, and the Longevity Chamber™ needs floor space of its own.

Does my staff treat patients in the program?

No. The program's clinicians evaluate and treat. Your team holds the consultations, schedules and handles administration.

Do my patients stay my patients?

For the care you provide, yes. For care delivered through the program, they are patients of the physician-owned practice, which keeps its own records.

How is my practice paid?

Your agreement sets it out, built for your state and your license. You read it in writing, with every fee, before you sign.

Will this conflict with my scope of practice?

It should not. You do not perform or direct the care. Your structure is set up for your state and your license, and you receive it in writing.

I am a physician. Does that change anything?

It can. A physician owner may be able to deliver care directly. Say so on the form and we will explain the options for your state.
