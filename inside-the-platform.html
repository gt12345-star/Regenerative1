A Look Inside the Platform

This is what you log into as a partner: your checklist, your website, your ordering, your training and the system that answers your phone.

TAKE THE NEXT STEP

From Your First Login to Your First Patient

Here are the screens, in the order you will meet them.

These screens are illustrations. The platform is still being finished, and the final screens may differ. You will see the real system before you decide anything.

Your first login

A welcome and a short checklist

Your account executive introduces themselves, and a checklist shows what to do first. Your agreements are signed on the screen, and you can see how far along you are.

  • A named account executive
  • Agreements signed online
  • Your progress at a glance

Your website

Your name, your design, your words

Pick a design and answer a few questions. The pages are drafted for you, and you can change any word or picture afterward. Every office has its own name and its own look, because you are an independent owner.

  • Several designs to choose from
  • Wording drafted for you
  • Yours to edit at any time

Ordering

Order and reorder from one screen

More than 20 biologics at partner pricing. Every order arrives with its lot documentation, and your order history is one tap away. A price sheet with your logo gives you a place to start.

  • More than 20 biologics
  • Lot documentation with every order
  • A price sheet under your name

Training

Training for your clinician and for your team

Your clinician gets injection training and coursework, with guided procedure videos. You and your team get sales training, scripts and practice conversations, so nobody takes a first consultation cold.

  • Injection training for your clinician
  • Sales training for you and your team
  • Practice conversations before the real ones

The assistant

Every call, text and website visit answered

Day and night, within seconds, the assistant explains how the office works and books the consultation. It keeps one conversation whether the person calls, texts or types, and it can pass a caller to your team. It gives no medical advice and quotes no prices.

  • Answers day and night
  • Books the consultation
  • No medical advice, no prices

Follow-up

People who did not book, brought back

Choose who to write to and what kind of message to send. The emails and texts are drafted for you, and a built-in check flags wording you should not send before anything goes out.

  • Emails and texts drafted for you
  • A wording check on every message
  • Your list, your timing

Your numbers

What the week brought in, on one screen

Inquiries, conversations and bookings in one place, so you can see what your advertising and your follow-up are doing.

  • Calls, texts and website conversations
  • Consultations booked
  • One login for all of it

How Patients Find Your Office

You run the local advertising. The platform answers every inquiry and follows up. These are samples of the kind of piece an office runs.

About these samples. The office name, the times and the phone numbers are placeholders. What you plan to say is reviewed before it runs.

What no piece will say

  • That a treatment will work
  • That it is approved by the FDA
  • Who is a candidate. A clinician decides that.

Questions About the Platform

All questions

Do I need to be technical?

No. Your website is drafted for you, your accounts are set up and tested for you, and your account executive walks you through each step.

Can I change my website after it is built?

Yes. You choose the name and the design, and you can change the wording and the pictures whenever you like.

Is this the finished platform?

The screens on this page are illustrations, and the platform is still being finished. You will be shown the real system on a call before you decide anything.

What does the platform cost after I open?

Every ongoing fee is itemized in the disclosure document, and you receive that document before you sign or pay anything.
