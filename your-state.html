Common Questions

All questions

Which states are open to partners?

All 50. We are placing a limited number of partnerships, so the question is whether one is still available. The partner brief will tell you.

Do the rules differ from state to state?

Yes. Your model is built for your state before you open, and you receive it in writing.

Do I need a medical license?

No. You own and operate a nonclinical management and marketing company. A licensed physician and clinical team are in place for you. They make all medical decisions and provide patient care. Your company does not practice medicine.

Do I need an office?

Yes. Partners generally operate from a small local office. We help you identify suitable space, including locations that already have furniture, telephones and internet service, so you can open without unnecessary startup expense. If you already run a practice, you have it.

Who makes sure it is compliant in my state?

We do, with healthcare counsel. Your structure is built for your state and delivered in writing.
