The opportunity

People now pay out of their own pockets to stay active, look younger and live longer. The Global Wellness Institute puts that market at $7.5 trillion, up 8.9 percent in a single year. The Census Bureau counts 61.2 million Americans age 65 and older, 13 percent more than in 2020, and many of them are asking about regenerative care.

You own the management and marketing company behind a local office. You hire the people who answer the phone, introduce the office to your community and take responsibility for how people are treated when they call. Licensed clinicians make every medical decision. You never diagnose anyone, choose a product or decide who is a candidate.

What stops most people who try

First come the healthcare attorneys, because the law decides who may own what. Then the search for a physician willing to supervise and a clinician trained to do the procedures. Then a supplier you can trust, a way to answer every call, and advertising that keeps everyone out of trouble. A year goes by, and so does a small fortune. Some never open at all.

All of it is handed to you

The partnership provides the legal structure for your state, physician oversight and a nurse practitioner for your office, with a staffing network behind that hire. Your supervising physician is named in writing.

You order from a catalog of more than 20 biologics at partner pricing. Your clinician is trained to perform the procedures, and you and your team are trained to hold the consultations. You choose the name, your website is drafted for you, and an assistant answers your phone, your texts and your website day and night.

What you own

The name on the sign is yours, the company is yours, and the accounts are in your company's name. Your phone is answered at midnight.

The investment

The minimum partner investment is $100,000. A local office is required. No medical license is required to own and operate the management company. Three numbers decide whether it is worth it to you: what a patient pays, what the physician and the treatment cost you, and how many patients it takes to cover what you put in. Ask us about all three. Financial performance information is provided in the disclosure document, in writing, and you will have that document before you sign or pay anything.

Common questions

Do I need a medical license?

No. You own the business: a management and marketing company. Licensed clinicians in a physician-owned practice make every clinical decision and deliver all care.

What does my office offer?

Regenerative care with biologics, chosen and performed by licensed clinicians, and sessions in a full-body red light chamber.

Who does the procedures?

A nurse practitioner in your office, trained through the program and working under physician oversight. You never perform, direct or choose a treatment.

Do I need an office?

Yes. Partners operate from a local office, where patients meet your team and your clinician in person. If you already run a practice, you have one.

What does the $100,000 cover?

A business that is already built: a compliant structure for your state, physician oversight, a clinician staffing network, the products, the training and the platform that runs the office. It is itemized in writing, with every ongoing fee, before you sign.

What does the platform do?

It gives your office a website under your own name, an AI assistant that answers calls, texts and website visitors day and night, a customer system, follow-up campaigns, training and ordering. One login.

What can I earn?

It comes down to three numbers: what a patient pays, what the physician and the treatment cost you, and how many patients cover your investment. Any financial performance information is provided in the disclosure document, in writing.

Are the stem cell therapies FDA-approved?

No. They have not been approved by the FDA. A licensed clinician evaluates each patient and decides whether treatment is appropriate.
