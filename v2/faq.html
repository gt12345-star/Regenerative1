Frequently Asked Questions

Ownership and Licensing

Do I need a medical license?

No. You own and operate a nonclinical management and marketing company. A licensed physician and clinical team are in place for you. They make all medical decisions and provide patient care. Your company does not practice medicine.

What exactly do I own?

The business: a management and marketing company, your office, your brand and your website. The medical practice is separate, with a physician in place for you.

Can a non-physician own the medical practice itself?

In most states, no, and you do not need to. A physician is in place for you on the medical side, and you own the business around it. We set that structure up for your state. Read the short guide.

Do I have to run it myself?

You run it, or you appoint a manager who does. It is not built to run without an owner.

The Menu

What can my patients be offered?

Regenerative care with biologics, chosen and performed by licensed clinicians, and sessions in the Longevity Chamber™. See what your office offers.

Who performs the procedures?

Qualified, state-licensed medical professionals, including nurse practitioners and physician assistants where authorized, working within their legal scope of practice and under the physician oversight state law requires. You never perform procedures, prescribe treatments or make medical decisions.

Do I have to find my own clinician?

No. Physician oversight is in place before you open, and a staffing network reaches all 50 states for the nurse practitioner in your office.

Where do the products come from?

From a catalog of more than 20 biologics, ordered at partner pricing. Every order arrives with its lot documentation.

Do I need an office?

Yes. Partners generally operate from a small local office. We help you identify suitable space, including locations that already have furniture, telephones and internet service, so you can open without unnecessary startup expense. If you already run a practice, you have it.

Do you offer red light therapy?

Yes. The Longevity Chamber™ is a full-body red and near-infrared light chamber that partners can offer at the office. It is a general wellness service, not a medical treatment, and we do not claim that it changes the result of any other treatment.

What does the AI platform do?

Think of it as the operating system for your business. It includes your own branded local website, an AI assistant that responds to calls, texts and website inquiries day and night, customer acquisition and marketing tools, automated follow-up campaigns, scheduling and administrative support, and business training and ordering tools. One integrated platform. One login. The assistant gives no medical advice.

Who is behind the clinical side?

A clinical network that reports more than 100 licensed clinicians, coverage in all 50 states and more than 6,000 patients treated. Your supervising physician is named in writing before you open.

Money

How much does it cost?

The minimum capital required is $100,000. The disclosure document itemizes the investment, along with every ongoing fee. Plan for working reserves on top.

How much can I earn?

It comes down to three numbers: what a patient pays, what the physician and the treatment cost you, and how many patients cover your investment. Any financial performance information comes in the written disclosure document.

How does my company get paid?

Your agreement sets it out, built for your state's rules. You read it in writing, with every fee, before you sign.

Why is there an investment up front?

You receive a built business: a compliant structure for your state, physician oversight, the products, the training and the platform that runs the office. The disclosure document itemizes every dollar before you sign.

What does the investment not cover?

Working capital, your office lease, your own team's payroll and anything not listed in your agreement.

What will I pay after the $100,000?

What any office pays: your team, your rent, your advertising and your product orders, plus the platform. The disclosure document itemizes every ongoing fee before you sign.

Compliance

Who makes sure it is compliant in my state?

We do, with healthcare counsel. Your structure is built for your state and delivered in writing.

Does this work in my state?

Yes. Partners are accepted in all 50 states. Your model is built for the state you operate in before you open, so you do not have to work out the rules yourself. Find your state.

Is this a franchise?

That is a legal question, and our attorneys answer it in writing. You receive the disclosure document and the waiting period the rules require before you sign or pay.

Are stem cell therapies FDA-approved?

The FDA has approved some stem cell products, for certain blood disorders. The therapies described on this website, for pain management, inflammation and general wellness, have not been approved by the FDA. A licensed medical professional independently evaluates each patient, explains the available options and decides whether any treatment is appropriate.

Do I need to understand the rules myself?

No. That is what you are paying us for. Your structure is built for your state, your marketing is cleared before it runs, and physicians make every clinical decision.

What happens when the rules change?

The affected part of your program is adjusted, and you are told in writing.

The Medicine and the Patients

What do physicians control?

Everything clinical: who is evaluated, who is a candidate, which product is used and whether to treat at all.

What will patients be told?

What the service is, who performs it and what it costs. They are also told that the therapies have not been approved by the FDA.

Who are the patients?

Adults who come in with questions about regenerative care. Whether anyone is a candidate is a clinical decision.

Where is care delivered?

In person, at your office, by a licensed clinician. How regenerative care is delivered is set up for your state before you open.

The Process

What happens after I request the brief?

You read it on your own time. If it fits, we hold a thirty-minute call. Then you receive the disclosure document, and the waiting period the law requires begins.

What happens between signing and opening?

Onboarding, step by step: your structure, your clinician, your website, your training and your first order. You open when each is in place.
