A Letter to a Future Partner

What you would own, what is handed to you, and the next step.

TAKE THE NEXT STEP

Dear future partner,

Become the local name people turn to for regenerative care

A doctor is looking for a non-doctor partner to operate a legal, compliant stem cell clinic in your city. You do not need a medical license. The clinicians, the legal structure, the products and the training are already in place. What is missing is a local owner.

The opportunity

The Global Wellness Institute puts the wellness market at $7.5 trillion, up 8.9 percent in a single year. The Census Bureau counts 61.2 million Americans age 65 and older, 13 percent more than in 2020, and many of them are asking about regenerative care.

You own the management and marketing company behind that office. You hire the people who answer the phone, introduce the office to your community and take responsibility for how people are treated when they call. When someone in town wants to know more, you hand them your card and tell them exactly what happens when they call. Licensed clinicians make every medical decision. You never diagnose anyone, choose a product or decide who is a candidate.

What stops most people who try

First come the healthcare attorneys, because the law decides who may own what. Then the search for a physician willing to supervise and a clinician trained to do the procedures. Then a supplier you can trust, a way to answer every call, and advertising that keeps everyone out of trouble. A year goes by, and so does a small fortune. Some never open at all.

All of it is handed to you

The partnership provides the legal structure for your state, physician oversight and a nurse practitioner for your office, with a staffing network that reaches all 50 states behind that hire. Your supervising physician is named in writing.

You order from a catalog of more than 20 biologics at partner pricing. Your clinician is trained to perform the procedures, and you and your team are trained to hold the consultations. A pricing playbook shows you how to package what your office offers.

You also receive the system that runs the business side. You choose the name, and your website is drafted for you to edit. An assistant answers your phone, your texts and your website day and night and books the consultations. Every inquiry lands in one place, and the follow-up is drafted for you.

Behind all of it stands a clinical network with more than 100 licensed clinicians, more than 6,000 patients treated and more than eight years in regenerative care.

What you own

The name on the sign is yours, the company is yours, and the accounts are in your company's name. When someone in town asks where to go for regenerative care, your office is the answer. Your phone is answered at midnight, and your patients have a reason to come back: twelve minutes in the Longevity Chamber™, offered as a membership.

The investment

The minimum capital required is $100,000. A local office is required. No medical license is required to own and operate the management company. Three numbers decide whether it is worth it to you: what a patient pays, what the physician and the treatment cost you, and how many patients it takes to cover what you put in. Ask us about all three. Financial performance information is provided in the disclosure document, in writing, and you will have that document before you sign or pay anything.

We are placing a limited number of partnerships. When they are placed, we stop taking applications. Not everyone who asks is accepted.

The next step

See whether you qualify. It takes about a minute, costs nothing and commits you to nothing. You then receive the partner brief: what you would own, what the $100,000 covers line by line, and what happens between signing and opening.

Nothing is signed and nothing is paid until you hold the full disclosure in writing and the waiting period the law requires has passed.

Sincerely,The partnership teamRegenerative Stem Cells Partnership

P.S. We are placing a limited number of partnerships, and when they are placed we stop taking applications. Find out whether one is still open.
