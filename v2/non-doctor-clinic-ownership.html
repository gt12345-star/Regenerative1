Can a Non-Doctor Own a Medical Clinic?

In most states the medical practice belongs to a physician. So a physician is in place for you, and you own the business around it. That is how our partners do it.

TAKE THE NEXT STEP

The Short Answer

A physician is in place for you on the medical side. You own a separate company that runs the business side: the office, the marketing, the scheduling and the team.

The details are different in every state. We set the structure up for yours, so you do not have to work them out.

What a Non-Doctor Owner Can and Cannot Do

You can

  • Own the office and the brand
  • Run marketing and hold consultations
  • Hire and manage non-clinical staff
  • Handle scheduling, billing support and administration

You cannot

  • Diagnose or treat a patient
  • Decide who is a candidate for care
  • Tell a clinician how to practice
  • Be paid by the patient or by the procedure

Common Questions

All questions

What is the corporate practice of medicine?

A rule in many states that only physicians, or companies they own, may practice medicine or employ physicians. It keeps business owners from directing medical care.

What is a management company?

A company that provides business services to a medical practice under a written agreement: the office, marketing, scheduling, staffing support, systems and administration. It is sometimes called an MSO.

Does this work in every state?

The structure is used across the country, and the details differ by state. That is why we set yours up for your state and give it to you in writing.

How is the management company paid?

That depends on the state, which is why your agreement is built for yours. You read how your company is paid, in writing, before you sign.

Do I need a medical director?

A physician must own or direct the medical practice. In this program, a named supervising physician is in place before you open.
