Common Questions

Do I need new space or equipment?

Not much. You already have the office. Regenerative care needs a room where a clinician can see patients, and the Longevity Chamber™ needs floor space of its own.

Does my staff treat patients in the program?

No. The program's clinicians evaluate and treat. Your team holds the consultations, schedules and handles administration.

How is my practice paid?

Your agreement sets it out, built for your state and your license. You read it in writing, with every fee, before you sign.

Are the stem cell therapies FDA-approved?

No. They have not been approved by the FDA. A licensed clinician evaluates each patient and decides whether treatment is appropriate. What your office offers, and how, is set up for your state before you open.

How much can I earn?

It comes down to three numbers: what a patient pays, what the physician and the treatment cost you, and how many patients cover your investment. Any financial performance information comes in the written disclosure document.
